Answer for: $ARGUMENTS

Use the `vendor-paperwork` skill and the `clausebench-registry` tools. Give the contracting legal entity, the
country, the transfer mechanism for the EEA and separately for the UK, and the links to the DPA, the
sub-processor list and the trust page, with the date a person last confirmed the record.

If the registry has no confident match, say so and stop. Do not substitute a link found elsewhere.
